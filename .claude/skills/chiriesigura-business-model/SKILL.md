# ChirieSigură monetization: check the fixed rules before proposing a fee

Landlords stay free; students pay a one-time €2.99. A percentage of rent does not fit: the rent never passes through the product, so a cut needs a rent record the product does not hold.

- Any landlord fee must be flat and charged before the checks run, whatever the result. It must at least cover the card fee and the Nokia calls.
- Every cost figure is an estimate until Nokia publishes per-call prices and the Stripe Romania pricing page is read.
- Rent data, fee arithmetic and the reasons: [references/business-model-analysis.md](references/business-model-analysis.md).
