It maps every 401 to `onExpired()`, which is the "re-verify your number" path. A signed-out renter now gets sent into number re-verification and never sees a sign-in prompt, so they cannot reach step 2 of the flow.
It maps every 403 to the `not_at_address` message. A barred reporter, or a landlord reporting their own listing, is told they are not at the address.
